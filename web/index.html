ABOUT THE PROJECT

Working with
Yorùbá sounds and tones

Yorùbá Phonemizer is an evolving language technology project. Its predictions are continually tested and refined.

Language review

A Yorùbá language expert positively reviewed 100 conversion outputs from the project. This review offers useful feedback, but does not establish the accuracy of every prediction.

Training and evaluation

The converter was trained on automatically generated pronunciation examples. Performance on a separate, independently verified test set has not yet been established.

Future speech support

Text-to-speech is a future direction. This version converts text to phonemes and does not generate audio.
