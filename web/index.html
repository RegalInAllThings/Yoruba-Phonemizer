LANGUAGE FIRST, TECHNOLOGY WITH CARE

Built with
Linguistic Expertise

Developed with attention to Yorùbá sounds, tone and orthography, and informed by expert review.

Expert perspective

A Yorùbá language expert positively reviewed 100 conversion outputs from the project. This is encouraging qualitative feedback; it does not establish the accuracy of every neural prediction shown here.

Transparent foundations

The GRU model learned from automatically generated silver labels. Independently verified held-out performance is not yet established. The reviewed converter is preserved in the project; this website serves the trained neural model directly.

A foundation for future speech

Text-to-speech is a future direction. This release focuses on text-to-phoneme exploration, with clear limits and no audio generation.
